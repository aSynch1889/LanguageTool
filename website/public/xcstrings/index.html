Batch-localize .xcstrings on Mac

LocaleDesk helps iOS teams translate and review String Catalog (.xcstrings) files without spreadsheet chaos.

What is .xcstrings?

Apple’s String Catalog format stores keys, comments, and per-locale translations in one structured file.

Why batch tooling helps

Hand-editing every locale is slow; spreadsheets often lose structure. LocaleDesk keeps a Convert → Review loop on Mac.

How LocaleDesk handles .xcstrings

  1. Convert — generate missing locales.
  2. Review — check keys before shipping.
  3. Export or sync — keep Xcode-friendly structure.

Who it’s for

Indie and small teams shipping multilingual iOS apps with String Catalogs.

Other formats

The same flow supports Flutter .arb and Electron .json.
